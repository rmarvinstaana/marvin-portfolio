How I Work

AI-native, by default

I don't just use AI tools, I build them. To run three brand accounts and eleven content pillars without a full content team, I designed and shipped the mb.io Content Dashboard: a scheduling and approval system, an AI writing agent trained on each brand's voice, a blog manager running a 100+ topic roadmap, and a pillar detection engine that flags coverage gaps before they happen.

The same logic runs through everything: AI video production (Higgsfield, Kling), agentic content workflows in Claude Code, and market monitoring pipelines that turn crypto, macro, and RWA feeds into a weekly Monday Outlook. The result is a one-person content operation with the output of a team.
